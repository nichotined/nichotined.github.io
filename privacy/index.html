Nexus Portal

Nexus Portal ("the app") is a personal productivity tool developed by Nicholas Frederich Lagaunne. It runs on your own computer.

Where your data lives

The app and its server run on your device. Your notes, chats, settings and connected-account tokens are stored only on your device. The developer doesn't operate a server for the app and doesn't receive, store or have access to your data.

Google user data

If you choose to connect your Google account, the app asks for access to:

  • Gmail: to read and search your email from within the app's tools.
  • Google Contacts: to look up your contacts from within the app's tools.

This data is requested directly from Google by the app running on your device, and is used only to show you results inside the app. It is not sent to the developer, sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else, except with an AI provider you've set up when you ask the AI chat to work with it (see below).

Nexus Portal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI chat

When you use AI chat, your messages, and any content you ask it to use, are sent to the AI provider you configured with your own account or API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or deleting its local data folder removes everything it stored. You can revoke the app's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Today Anime

Today Anime is a mobile app developed by Nicholas Frederich Lagaunne for discovering anime and anime news.

No personal data collected

Today Anime doesn't collect, store or share any personally identifiable information.

Third-party ads

The app may show ads from third-party providers such as Google AdMob. These providers may use cookies or device identifiers to deliver ads, and may collect some data as part of that. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are set up to be appropriate for all ages, and personalized ads are not shown to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
