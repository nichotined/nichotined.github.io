What it does

Workflow

Keep your daily tasks and tools together instead of across a dozen tabs.

Notes

Write and organize notes, stored as files on your own machine.

AI chat

Chat with AI next to your notes and tools, with your own context.

MCP servers & plugins

Add your own MCP servers and plugins once, then use their tools from one place.

Your data stays on your machine

Nexus Portal starts its own server on your computer. There's no cloud account, and nothing is sent to me.
